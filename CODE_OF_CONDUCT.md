# Code of Conduct

Split-It-Wise follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short, it asks everyone taking part in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other
project space to:

- be welcoming and respectful, whatever someone's background or experience
- give and take feedback on the work, not the person
- assume good intent, and own up to mistakes
- never harass, insult, or publish anyone's private information

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above and applies
here in full.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately at the email on
[@aashu2006's GitHub profile](https://github.com/aashu2006). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or ban a participant,
in line with the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
